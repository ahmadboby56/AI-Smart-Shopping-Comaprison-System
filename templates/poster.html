Final Year Project

SmartShopping

AI-Powered Price Comparison Platform

An advanced web aggregation and analytics platform designed to solve e-commerce fragmentation in Pakistan. The system fetches real-time prices concurrently from local and international stores, calculates merchant reliability scores using natural language sentiment analysis, tracks long-term historical price fluctuations, and triggers automatic email alerts on target price drops.

Project Context

⚠️ Problem

Online consumers in Pakistan suffer from market fragmentation. Product listings vary drastically in cost, shipping, and quality across e-commerce portals. Visiting multiple sites is exhaustingly slow, often leading to overpayment or purchasing from untrustworthy platforms without seller verification.

✅ Solution

A consolidated price comparison engine driving concurrent scrape pipelines across 11 platforms. Aggregates offers instantly, computes merchant reliability with active NLP Sentiment Scoring of user feedback, logs historic pricing, and triggers background email notifications on price drops.
