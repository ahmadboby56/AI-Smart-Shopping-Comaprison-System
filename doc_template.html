📌 Executive Project Overview

Smart Shopping is an intelligent, full-stack e-commerce aggregation and voice-assisted decision engine. It unifies product listings across 10+ major online marketplaces (Daraz.pk, PriceOye, Telemart, Mega.pk, Ubuy, Amazon, etc.) in real time. Powered by multi-accent acoustic speech recognition, backend Levenshtein fuzzy string resolution, and TextBlob NLP sentiment analysis, Smart Shopping empowers users to discover transparent pricing, evaluate seller credibility, track historical price volatility, and save time & money.

🚀 Core AI & Technical Innovations Technical Deep-Dive

🎙️ Multi-Accent Voice AI

Integrates Web Speech API with dynamic browser locale detection (navigator.language) and multi-alternative evaluation (maxAlternatives=5). Accurately transcribes South Asian, Middle Eastern, and Western English accents.

🔤 Levenshtein Fuzzy Search

Backend fault-tolerance powered by Python's difflib. Automatically resolves voice transcription errors or phonetic typos (e.g. "airburds" → "AirPods Pro"), ensuring zero zero-result search failures.

🛡️ NLP Merchant Trust Rating

Evaluates seller review sentiment via TextBlob, price volatility ratios, return policy terms, and return rate metrics to compute an automated 0–100 Trust Score badge per product.

🌐 Marketplace Coverage & Aggregation Engine

🛍️ Supported E-Commerce Marketplaces

Scrapes and aggregates live listings from Daraz.pk, PriceOye, Telemart, Mega.pk, Surmawala, Ubuy, Shophive, Clicky, Amazon, and eBay. Built-in request retries and header rotation prevent IP rate-limiting.

📈 Price Volatility & Alert Notification

Tracks every product query in SQLite time-series logs. Interactive Chart.js graphs display 7-day and 30-day price trends, alerting users when a item reaches its historical lowest price point.

💼 Monetization Model & Admin Analytics

💰 Affiliate Revenue Model

Outbound merchant clicks embed dynamic tracking parameters. The platform calculates estimated affiliate revenue (standard 3% baseline commission) per converted redirect click to demonstrate commercial sustainability.

📊 Administrative Monitoring Dashboard

Secured portal (/admin-login) providing real-time tracking of top searched keywords, conversion metrics, scraping latency logs, and user search volume distributions across categories.

🌟 Key Market Value & Innovation Highlights

  • Democratizing E-Commerce in Emerging Markets: Solves price opacity and fragmented product availability across local South Asian retail platforms.
  • Accessibility via Voice AI: Enables voice-based search for non-fluent or non-typing users with natural multi-accent phoneme handling.
  • AI-Driven Consumer Protection: Protects consumers from fake reviews and inflated "discounted" prices through NLP Trust Scores and historical price trend validation.
  • High Scalability: Lightweight Python micro-architecture deployable on cloud containers (Render/Docker) with sub-second aggregate responses.
